# Shopee & TikTok Live Stream Script Generator

Drafts 3-minute operational retention loops for live stream hosts.

## 3-Minute Loop Structure
* **0-30s**: Hero product introduction + limited discount callout.
* **30s-2m**: Physical product demonstration & live Q&A handling.
* **2m-3m**: Flash voucher drop + urgent stock callout (*"Klik keranjang kuning sekarang!"*).

## Engagement Mechanics
1. **Countdown urgency**: use platform's countdown features, not fake scarcity claims.
2. **Comment keywords**: one keyword per loop (*"ketik HARGA"*) — measurable & simple.
3. **Cross-sell**: bundle item mention once per loop, no hard sell.
4. **Interaction recovery**: script 2-3 fallback questions to re-engage silent viewers.

## Scope & Safety
* **Use for**: live shopping scripts (Shopee Live, TikTok Live), giveaway routines.
* **Do not use for**: fake stock claims (*"tinggal 1!"* padahal banyak), harga pembanding fiktif, atau klaim khasiat — pelanggaran platform policy & UU 8/1999.
* **Gimmick discipline**: giveaway/undian wajib mengikuti syarat promosi platform; sebutkan syarat & ketentuan singkat di screen.

## Worked Example
Input: `product: "mixer mini portable 400W" / discount: "25%" / campaign: "Flash sale 21.00"`
Loop 1: *"Kita buka flash sale 21.00, mixer 400W cuma 25% off — ketik 'MIXER' kalau mau dapet info stok."* → demo 60 detik (putar jus) → *"Stok flash sale dibuka, klik keranjang kuning sekarang!"* → tanggapi 1 pertanyaan chat → ulangi loop dengan produk bundling spatula.
