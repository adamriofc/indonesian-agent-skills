# Executive Storytelling & Thread Writer

Formulates business metrics, failures, or growth milestones into engaging LinkedIn posts and X threads.

## Formulas
* **LinkedIn Storytelling**: Hook (Metric/Failure) -> Story (Pivots/Struggle) -> 3 Takeaways.
* **X Thread Outline**: Tweet 1 (Hook + 🧵), Tweets 2-5 (Process), Tweet 6 (CTA).

## Indonesian Audience Nuances
1. Hook memakai angka konkret atau konflik lokal (*"Gagal 3x buka toko online, untungnya…"*).
2. Keep paragraphs 1-2 lines; use line-breaks, not dense walls — mobile-first readers.
3. No humble-brag overload: bukti > klaim; sertakan screenshot data bila ada.
4. CTA realistic: "Simpan thread ini" / "Follow untuk…" — hindari janji hasil instan.

## Scope & Safety
* **Use for**: personal branding, company milestone announcements, thought leadership.
* **Do not use for**: insider-sensitive data (belum publik), rahasia dagang, atau klaim keuangan tak terverifikasi — pelanggaran bisa berujung sanksi perusahaan & sengketa.
* **Disclosure**: posting promosi/afiliasi wajib menyatakan hubungan komersial.

## Worked Example
Input: `case: "UMKM naikkan penjualan 40% dalam 3 bulan via repricing" / takeaways: ["data rutin", "uji harga", "CS follow-up"]`
Hook: *"Penjualan naik 40% dalam 3 bulan tanpa naik budget iklan."* → Story: proses repricing berbasis data 2 mingguan → 3 takeaways bullet → CTA: *"Komen 'DATA' kalau mau template tabelnya."*
